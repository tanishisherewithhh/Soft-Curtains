# **Soft Curtains**
Soft Curtains is a fabric mod to add many types of curtains to your world
</div>
<br>

Currently, it includes [Curtain Drapes](), [Shutters](), [Blinds](), and [Roller]() styles.
It also adds [Curtain Rods]() and a [Tailoring Sheer]().

> These curtains are each available in 16 different colors, and the rods are available in all wood, iron, gold and copper variants!

These curtains use low cost cloth-like physics using a mix of pendulum dynamics and mass-spring system which are stitched together to make the curtains appear "soft" rather than rigid.

- `Right clicking` on a rod with a curtain will place the curtain on the rod. The direction of the curtain depends on which "edge" of the rod you were looking at.
> Meaning, if I place the curtain while I look at the left edge of the rod, the curtain will open towards the right side.
- Similar curtains are "connected" together when placed in the same direction adjacent to each other. This allows you to have curtains of indefinite horizontal length controlled together.

### How to use

| Action                           | Control                                                                   |
|:---------------------------------|:--------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Manual pulling the curtains      | Hold Right-Click and drag the mouse horizontally                          |
| Instant toggle the curtains      | Shift + Right-Click                                                       |
| Cycle curtain styles             | Right-Click with Tailoring Shears                                         |
| Increase vertical curtain length | Right-Click with wool (adds that wool's color)                            |
| Decrease vertical curtain length | Right-Click with standard shears                                          |
| Change the rod's blockstate      | Shift + Right-Click on a curtain rod (the rod must not have any curtains) |

---

### Redstone

Power any curtain rod with redstone to open or close the whole curtain. Higher signal strength increases the movement speed.
